Tripo character meshes (free-plan terms, not CC BY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Character meshes were generated with the Tripo free plan (H2.5, untextured)
from original concept art, then decimated in this project and given stylised
PBR sub-materials (skin, hair, robe, trim, shoes, gold, beads, hat, dragon).
Used with attribution under Tripo free-plan terms. Do not describe these as
CC BY (that licence is Meshy's). Do not replace these with the unoptimized
high-poly sources, which stay outside the student-facing game folder.
